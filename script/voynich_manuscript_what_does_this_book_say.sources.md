# Voynich Manuscript: What Does This Book Actually Say?

Research date: 26 October 2026.

Companion narration: `voynich_manuscript_what_does_this_book_say.txt`.

The narration is original English prose intended for speech generation. It contains no headings, citation markers, production directions, invented dialogue, or purported translations. Paragraph numbers below count nonempty paragraphs in the text file. The central conclusion is that no decipherment of the main text has gained general scholarly acceptance in the sources checked.

## Editorial decisions and limits

- Research includes primary laboratory evidence, an archival-letter excerpt, institutional sources, original research, and author descriptions. Abstract-only access is identified above. Search results and later headlines were discovery aids, not substitutes for a translated manuscript.
- Exact surviving page and word totals are omitted. Published accounts differ in how they count folios, pages, foldouts, and transcription units.
- Radiocarbon dating does not directly date the ink. The original report's conclusion and Figure 7 take precedence over simplified news summaries.
- Section names describe illustrations. The narration does not identify a plant species, declare the book a medical manual, or assign a meaning to a Voynich token.
- Five scribal hands are an attributed paleographic finding. Institutional affiliation and the division between copying and composition remain unknown.
- The linguistic, meaningless-text, Naibbe, and page-order proposals answer different questions. They are not presented as equal demonstrations, a consensus, or an exhaustive list of hypotheses.
- Paragraphs 6, 12, 15, 17, 19, and 21–27 include explanatory reasoning and editorial standards. Their questions, analogies, and closing reflections add no invented observation or recovered speech. The suggested tests for a decipherment are editorial methodological guidance, not an official Yale certification procedure.
- No claim is made that AI has translated the book, that every possible cipher has been eliminated, or that the manuscript contains supernatural knowledge. No proposed translation is reproduced as fact.
- Research status is bounded by sources checked on 26 October 2026. Manuscript custodianship and scholarly acceptance are separate: the library's ownership does not make it an adjudicator of every proposed reading.
